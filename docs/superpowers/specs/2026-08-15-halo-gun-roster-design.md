# RIFTLANE Sandbox v1 — "Fidelity, Corrected"

_Design spec from the 9-agent ultracode tournament, 2026-08-15._

**STATUS: SHIPPED, except E12.** The roster, the engine deltas E1-E11, the
fixed spawn loadout and the map pad tables are all implemented and tested.

**E12 (bot pad-seeking) was built, measured, and NOT shipped.** It collapsed
bastion from ~150 kills and 1-2 captures per match to 12 kills and zero, on
every seed. Root cause is a Navigator/teleporter interaction, not a weapons
problem; full autopsy in `docs/ERRORS.md` (2026-08-15). The consequence is
the one this document warned about: bots fight with the spawn pair and the
niche/power tiers are a human-facing layer for now.

Two deviations from the spec below, both deliberate:
- The projectile kind literal was renamed `ion_charge` -> `cinderlob` rather
  than left in place. Leaving it was half a rename, and the client reads that
  literal to pick the projectile's visuals.
- The Cindershot splashes on ANY detonation, not only a direct player hit.
  A round that bursts on the wall beside a defender has to do something, or
  the anti-cover weapon is just a worse rocket.

## Philosophy

A player who has played Halo Infinite must recognize every gun in RIFTLANE by feel within two seconds of pulling the trigger. That is the winning lens, and the roster keeps its structure whole: one fixed spawn pair every life (MA40 + MK50), a niche tier on short pads, a power tier on long neutral pads, and a headshot reward that is a GRADIENT scaled by weapon class rather than a flat bonus — 0.10s on the AR, 0.13s on the Sidekick, 0.33s on the BR, and a full one-shot on the Sniper.

Three corrections were grafted in from the losing rosters and from my own read of the source, and all three are load-bearing.

(1) EVERY RATE OF FIRE IS RE-SNAPPED TO THE 30 Hz TICK GRID. `MatchSim.tick` advances `now` by exactly TICK_DT and `stepFire` sets `cooldownUntil = now + 1/rof`, so the real shot interval is `ceil(30/rof)` ticks, not `1/rof`. The tournament's headline numbers were fiction: fidelity's rof 9 AR is 7.5/s and kills in 1.600s, not 1.333s; ctf_pacing's rof 12 SMG is 10/s. Every rof in this table is 30/n for an integer n, so the TTK column means what it says, and one epsilon on the cooldown gate makes the boundary deterministic against float drift in `simNow += TICK_DT`.

(2) THE ROCKET BUG IS FIXED AT THE GEOMETRY, NOT WITH A DAMAGE NUMBER. `stepProjectile` detonates within PLAYER_BODY_RADIUS 0.58 of `bodyCenter` (feet + 0.9), while `explode()` measures falloff from `p.pos` — the FEET. That is why today's 120/3.0 SPNKR does ~77 on a clean direct hit (worst case 60.8, not the 64 fidelity claimed) and cannot one-shot anyone. fidelity answered with 190 damage, which halo_feel correctly called an over-serve with a 2.13m no-LOS kill sphere; ctf_pacing and bot_first answered with 150, which ctf_math correctly showed is a coin flip at 100.7 against a 100 pool. Both were treating a symptom. The fix is three lines in `explode()`: clamp the blast's Y to the target's own body column before measuring, so falloff runs to the nearest point of the player rather than to their shoes. Then 150/4.5 gives 130.7 on a guaranteed direct hit with 30 points of margin, a clean 1.5m lethal bubble, and — critically — frag and mag grenades keep their current tuning exactly, which a naive "measure to chest" fix would have quietly nerfed by 20 points.

(3) THE PLASMA PISTOL IS CUT AND THE CINDERSHOT TAKES ITS PROJECTILE SLOT. `kind: 'charge'` is a label; `stepFire` runs it through byte-identical code to `'projectile'` and there is no hold-to-charge mechanic anywhere in the engine. A Plasma Pistol that cannot overcharge is the one gun in fidelity's roster that fails its own two-second recognition test. The Cindershot is a real Infinite weapon this engine CAN express in full, and it takes the slot for two changed lines: keep the `'ion_charge'` Projectile kind literal so all four switch sites keep compiling, and swap `explodeProjectile`'s flat `applyDamage` for `explode()`. The surrounding aliveBefore/killPlayer loop already handles multi-victim kills. That also buys the sandbox its missing shoot-behind-cover tool and a regen-suppression lane.

Two more grafts land: the Gravity Hammer finally becomes a Hammer (an `aoeMelee` flag turning `doMeleeAttack`'s single-best-target pick into a loop — the AOE IS the weapon, and all three rosters either cut it or shipped it as a worse Sword), and power pads move onto the route BETWEEN bases, never inside a flag room, because the same OHK melee that reads as a push tool at mid reads as an unbreakable flag camp where a defender already stands.

One thing is stated plainly rather than papered over: `stepPickups` carries the comment "Bots ignore pads for now — they never path to one." I grepped all of server/src/bots and confirmed there is zero pad logic. Until delta E12 lands, the niche and power tiers are a HUMAN-ONLY layer and every seeded bot match is fought with AR + Sidekick. That is not a footnote; it is the difference between an 11-weapon sandbox and a 2-weapon one, and no roster in the bracket except ctf_pacing was willing to say it.

## Weapons

### pulse_smg — MA40 Assault Rifle (MA40 AR)

- **Role:** Spawn primary, slot 0. Forgiving close-to-mid spray that wins point-blank scrambles and never leaves a player disarmed. Held more than any other gun in the game, so its TTK is the sandbox's baseline.
- **Kind:** hitscan
- **Stats:** `{"damage":8,"headshotMult":1.5,"rof":10,"magSize":32,"pellets":1,"spread":0.055,"maxRange":45}`
- **TTK math:** rof 10 = 3 ticks = 0.1000s exact on the 30Hz grid. BODY: ceil(100/8) = 13 shots; first at t=0, thirteenth at 12 x 0.1 = 1.200s. HEAD under the shield gate: shots 1-8 deal 64, shield 70 -> 6. Shot 9 reads target.shield = 6 > 0, so mult is 1 even on a head hit: 8 damage zeroes the shield and spills 2 into health, leaving 28. From shot 10 the gate opens and heads pay 8 x 1.5 = 12; ceil(28/12) = 3 more. Kill on shot 12 at 11 x 0.1 = 1.100s. Head buys 0.100s — deliberately the smallest reward in the roster, because an AR is not a precision weapon and this is the gun bots hold. SUSTAIN GATE: 80 DPS vs SHIELD_RECHARGE_RATE 35/s (pass); 32 x 8 = 256 body damage per mag = 2.56 kills, 3.1s of continuous fire, so a push never eats RELOAD_TIME mid-duel. NOTE vs the tournament: fidelity specced rof 9, which quantizes to 4 ticks = 7.5/s and a real 1.600s body TTK — a defense buff shipped into a defense-favoured stalemate. rof 10 is the current live value and it is grid-exact.
- **Bots:** Best-case bot weapon and the one they will hold in nearly every seeded match (see the pad blocker). Hitscan, so zero lead prediction needed. 13 damage rolls per kill means the bot's 4-degree Gaussian error averages out instead of deciding the fight, and spread 0.055 hides that error inside 20m. Bots aim at chestPos and never at the head, so their real AR TTK is the 1.200s body number — the correct handicap. REQUIRES the weaponRangeScore fix: maxRange 45 means a bot plinking across a 40m Bastion sightline does literally nothing, and today the AR and the Sidekick both score 1 in the LONG bracket, so desiredWeaponSlot ties and the bot keeps the capped AR out. Add sidearm to the LONG score-3 branch.
- **Viewmodel:** rifleParts(false). Flip the compact test at viewmodel.ts:217 from `id === 'sidearm' || id === 'pulse_smg'` to `id === 'sidearm'` so the AR reads full-size — a compact AR silhouette is the first thing that breaks the two-second recognition test. No new geometry.
- **Engine deltas:** None. WeaponDef.name string, stat fields, and the one-token compact-flag edit in viewmodel.ts.

### sidearm — MK50 Sidekick (MK50 Sidekick)

- **Role:** Spawn secondary, slot 1, on every player and every bot. The precision half of the utility pair and the game's best shield-down cleanup tool.
- **Kind:** hitscan
- **Stats:** `{"damage":12,"headshotMult":2,"rof":7.5,"magSize":12,"pellets":1,"spread":0.012,"maxRange":60}`
- **TTK math:** rof 7.5 = 4 ticks = 0.13333s exact. BODY: ceil(100/12) = 9 shots; 8 x 0.13333 = 1.067s. HEAD: shots 1-5 deal 60, shield 70 -> 10. Shot 6 reads shield 10 > 0 so mult is 1: 12 damage zeroes the shield, 2 spills, health 28. Shots 7 and 8 land heads at 24 each = 48 >= 28. Kill on shot 8 at 7 x 0.13333 = 0.933s. Fastest TTK of the utility tier in both columns, and that is the Halo point exactly: the Sidekick out-duels the AR (0.933 vs 1.100 on heads, 1.067 vs 1.200 on bodies) only for a player who can hold the head line on a 0.012 cone, and it pays for the speed with 12 rounds and no room to spray. Mag covers the 9-shot body kill with 3 to spare. SUSTAIN: 90 DPS, 144 body damage per mag.
- **Bots:** Guaranteed in every bot's slot 1 and correctly weak in their hands. Chest-only aim caps a bot at the 1.067s body number, so a human who can head-tap always out-duels a bot holding the same gun — the cleanest human/bot asymmetry in the roster, and it costs nothing to build. spread 0.012 gives no forgiveness, so bots land it inside ~12m and miss beyond, which matches its cleanup role. Add `sidearm` to the LONG_RANGE score-3 branch so a bot at 30m swaps off the 45m-capped AR onto this instead of tying and holding the AR.
- **Viewmodel:** rifleParts(true) — the compact variant, unchanged. It stays the only weapon using compact after the AR is promoted to full-size, which is exactly the silhouette contrast a sidearm needs.
- **Engine deltas:** None. Stat fields only (damage 15->12, rof 4->7.5, magSize 12 unchanged, spread 0.01->0.012, maxRange added at 60).

### triad_rifle — BR75 Battle Rifle (BR75)

- **Role:** Niche pad, 30s, mirrored one per side. The mid-range staple and the widest precision reward in the sandbox. Punishes anyone caught in the open at 15-40m.
- **Kind:** burst
- **Stats:** `{"damage":8,"headshotMult":2,"rof":3,"magSize":36,"pellets":3,"spread":0.015,"maxRange":120}`
- **TTK math:** rof 3 = 10 ticks = 0.33333s exact. pellets 3 fires three independently jittered rays in ONE tick — there is no intra-burst stagger in stepFire — but the shield gate is read PER RAY before applyDamage, so a burst can strip the shield with ray 2 and land a multiplied finisher with ray 3. Burst = 24 body. BODY: 24/48/72/96/120 -> 5 bursts, 4 x 0.33333 = 1.333s. HEAD: burst 1 -> shield 46. Burst 2 -> shield 22. Burst 3: ray1 -> 14, ray2 -> 6, ray3 reads shield 6 > 0 so mult 1, zeroes the shield with 2 spilling, health 28. Burst 4: ray1 now reads shield <= 0 and pays 8 x 2 = 16 -> health 12; ray2 pays 16 -> dead. Kill lands inside burst 4, fired at 3 x 0.33333 = 1.000s. Heads 1.000s vs body 1.333s: a 0.333s reward, the widest in the roster and the reason this is a pad weapon rather than a spawn weapon. Mag 36 = 12 bursts. SUSTAIN: 72 DPS, 288 body damage per mag. NOTE: fidelity specced rof 2.5 (grid-exact, 12 ticks) for 1.600s/1.200s; that body number is slower than Infinite's BR and slower than the AR it is supposed to out-range, so rof moves to 3.
- **Bots:** No special-casing — the burst branch is the same pellet loop as hitscan. Bots already score triad_rifle 3 in the LONG bracket, so a bot that walks over a BR pad correctly holds it on long sightlines. All three rays resolve from one aim solution per tick, so the bot's per-tick error is sampled once per burst rather than three times: a bot that is on target lands all three. spread 0.015 is tighter than the bot's own 4-degree error, so accuracy is bounded by the bot, not the gun. Bots never collect the 2x, so their BR is a flat 1.333s weapon.
- **Viewmodel:** Unchanged. This is the one weapon with a bespoke async GLB (`/assets/models/viewmodel-rifle.glb`, RIFLE_GLB_SCALE 0.6) that hides the procedural rifleParts fallback. Do not touch the loader or the offsets; the rename to 'BR75 Battle Rifle' is a WeaponDef.name string and the model is keyed off the id.
- **Engine deltas:** None. Stat fields only (rof 1.5->3, damage 12->8, magSize 24->36, spread 0.02->0.015, maxRange added at 120). NOT RECOMMENDED for v1: a true intra-burst stagger would need a scheduled-shot queue in stepFire; the simultaneous burst already produces the correct strip-then-multiplied-finisher behavior inside one trigger pull, which is what the weapon feels like.

### commando — VK78 Commando (VK78 Commando)

- **Role:** Niche pad, 30s, one neutral. Full-auto precision rifle that bridges AR spray and BR range: more forgiving than the BR, slower to kill, reaches 90m and carries 30 rounds.
- **Kind:** hitscan
- **Stats:** `{"damage":10,"headshotMult":2,"rof":6,"magSize":30,"pellets":1,"spread":0.03,"maxRange":90}`
- **TTK math:** rof 6 = 5 ticks = 0.16667s exact. BODY: ceil(100/10) = 10 shots; 9 x 0.16667 = 1.500s — the slowest gun in the roster, on purpose. HEAD: this is the clean case. 70/10 = exactly 7 shots to zero the shield with ZERO overflow (shot 7 reads shield 10 > 0, so mult 1, and 10 damage lands the shield on exactly 0 without touching health, which stays at 30). Shot 8 now reads shield <= 0 and pays 20 -> health 10; shot 9 pays 20 -> dead. 9 shots at 8 x 0.16667 = 1.333s. Strictly worse than the BR's 1.000s and the AR's 1.100s, which is the exact Infinite tradeoff: the Commando is what you take when you cannot hold a burst rhythm and you need range plus a magazine. SUSTAIN: 60 DPS, 300 body damage per mag — the deepest magazine in the roster.
- **Bots:** The best bot weapon on any pad: full-auto hitscan, no lead, no burst timing, and a generous 0.03 cone that hides Gaussian aim error. Add `commando` to the weaponRangeScore LONG_RANGE score-3 branch alongside railspike and triad_rifle, or bots at 30m will rate it 1 and never swap onto it. Bots get the flat 1.500s body number.
- **Viewmodel:** rifleParts(false), the full-size procedural rifle. Its accent hue comes from accentColor(), which is why delta E10 (fixed divisor) must land before this id is appended to WEAPON_ORDER — otherwise adding it re-hues every existing weapon's viewmodel accent AND its pickup-pad glow/hologram. No new geometry needed; if it ever wants to read distinct from the AR, give it the same async-GLB treatment triad_rifle already has rather than a new procedural family.
- **Engine deltas:** The only genuinely new WeaponId in the roster, and it is hitscan, so it touches zero projectile plumbing — no Projectile['kind'], no case in projectileKindForWeapon / weaponIdForProjectileKind / explodeProjectile. Touches: WeaponId union in types.ts, WEAPONS entry, WEAPON_POOL, WEAPON_ORDER append in viewmodel.ts (after E10), weaponRangeScore LONG branch in brain.ts, and the map pad tables.

### scattergun — CQS48 Bulldog (CQS48 Bulldog)

- **Role:** Niche pad, 45s, mirrored in the side halls. Close-range room-clearer, hard-capped at 15m so it can never contest a lane.
- **Kind:** hitscan
- **Stats:** `{"damage":12,"headshotMult":1.5,"rof":1.5,"magSize":7,"pellets":8,"spread":0.16,"maxRange":15}`
- **TTK math:** rof 1.5 = 20 ticks = 0.66667s exact. 8 independently jittered rays per trigger pull. BODY MAXIMUM IS 8 x 12 = 96 < 100, so a body blast PROVABLY cannot one-tap from full shield — that is the single most important number here, because Infinite's Bulldog is not a one-shot and because bots auto-swap to this under 4m. POINT BLANK (8 pellets): 96 zeroes the shield and spills 26, leaving health 4; shot 2 kills at 0.667s. At ~8m (5 pellets, 60/shot): 60 then 120 -> still 2 shots, 0.667s. At ~12m (4 pellets, 48/shot): 48/96/144 -> 3 shots, 1.333s. HEAD, honest edge case: pellets 1-5 deal 60 (shield 10); pellet 6 reads shield 10 > 0 so mult 1, zeroes it, 2 spills, health 28; pellets 7 and 8 landing on the head pay 18 each = 36 >= 28, so a blast centred on the head at contact range DOES kill in one pull. That is Bulldog-correct and it requires the head sphere (radius 0.30) to eat two of eight pellets, which the 0.16 cone makes a contact-range-only outcome. maxRange 15 is the falloff proxy: the engine has no damage falloff, so a hard raycast cap is the only lane tool available. NOTE: fidelity specced 15 damage, which makes 7 of 8 pellets = 105 a full-shield one-tap in the hands of any bot that walks into a hallway — an unpriced lethality spike engine_fit flagged. 12 removes it by arithmetic.
- **Bots:** Bots use it well inside its lane: weaponRangeScore already gives scattergun 2 in the CLOSE bracket, so they swap to it in corridors, and a 0.16 cone (9.2 degrees half-angle) is more than double their 4-degree error, so partial pellet hits still deal partial damage instead of a whiff. REQUIRES the `scattergun -> 0` line in the LONG bracket: today it falls through to the generic 1, so a bot holding a 15m-capped shotgun at 30m will keep it out and fire into empty air. That is a bug fix, not a tune.
- **Viewmodel:** rifleParts(false), unchanged.
- **Engine deltas:** None. Stat fields only (damage 12 unchanged, headshotMult 2->1.5, rof 1.2->1.5, magSize 6->7, spread 0.18->0.16, maxRange 25->15). Plus the one-line weaponRangeScore LONG fix, which belongs to brain.ts.

### swarm_pod — Needler (Needler)

- **Role:** Niche pad, 60s, neutral mid. Homing anti-shield shredder with a supercombine payoff. The one weapon that punishes a strafing flag carrier, and the most bot-competent pad weapon in the roster.
- **Kind:** projectile
- **Stats:** `{"damage":7,"headshotMult":1,"rof":7.5,"magSize":24,"pellets":1,"spread":0.05,"projectileSpeed":24,"homing":true}`
- **TTK math:** rof 7.5 = 4 ticks = 0.13333s exact. Two damage sources: 7 flat per stick via explodeProjectile's swarm_dart case, plus SWARM_POP_DAMAGE 80 once stuckDarts reaches SWARM_POP_THRESHOLD 6 (checkSwarmPop fires after the sixth dart's own applyDamage and resets the counter). SUPERCOMBINE: sticks 1-5 deal 35, shield 70 -> 35. Stick 6 deals 7 -> shield 28, then the pop applies 80: 28 absorbed, 52 spills into 30 health -> DEAD on the sixth needle. Trigger time for 6 = 5 x 0.13333 = 0.667s; add flight (24 m/s = 0.417s at 10m) for a real ~1.08s at 10m and ~1.29s at 15m. WITHOUT the pop it is nearly harmless: 5 sticks is 35 damage, and a pure stick kill needs ceil(100/7) = 15 needles = 14 x 0.13333 = 1.867s plus flight. The pop IS the weapon, exactly as in Infinite, and breaking line of sight before the sixth needle costs the shooter everything. headshotMult stays 1 — projectiles deal flat contact damage with no head/body raycast distinction. Mag 24 = four supercombines.
- **Bots:** The best bot-friendly pad weapon, and it works purely because of homing. findHomingTarget locks at spawn inside the 30-degree HOMING_CONE_ANGLE and HOMING_TURN_RATE 4 rad/s steers in flight, which papers over exactly the lead prediction computeAim does not have. A bot firing at a strafing target's current chestPos still connects and still pops at 10m. Score it 2 in the CLOSE and mid brackets.
- **Viewmodel:** launcherParts(), unchanged — it is kind 'projectile'. The accent hue is the only per-weapon differentiation; keep the id in its existing WEAPON_ORDER position so its hologram color does not move.
- **Engine deltas:** None of its own. Speed 20->24 is a plain field, but it makes delta E5 (swept projectile collision) load-bearing: stepProjectile point-samples distSq(pr.pos, bodyCenter) against PLAYER_BODY_RADIUS 0.58 after advancing pos by vel*dt, so at 24 m/s a needle moves 0.8m per tick against a 1.16m-diameter sphere and a glancing trajectory can pass clean through. Six discrete sticks are required for the pop, so dropped sticks are not cosmetic here. Either ship E5 or hold projectileSpeed at 20.

### cinderlob — Cindershot (Cindershot)

- **Role:** Niche pad, 60s, neutral mid. REPLACES ion_charger. The anti-cover tool: the only weapon that reliably damages a defender who is not in clean line of sight, and the only one that can deny shield regeneration to a whole flag room from behind a wall.
- **Kind:** projectile
- **Stats:** `{"damage":70,"headshotMult":1,"rof":1.25,"magSize":5,"pellets":1,"spread":0,"projectileSpeed":22,"splashRadius":3.5,"homing":true}`
- **TTK math:** rof 1.25 = 24 ticks = 0.80000s exact. All numbers below assume delta E3 (explode() measures to the nearest point of the target's body column instead of their feet). DIRECT HIT: stepProjectile detonates within PLAYER_BODY_RADIUS 0.58 of bodyCenter, so worst-case d = 0.58 -> 70 x (1 - 0.58/3.5) = 58.4; typical d ~0.3 -> 64.0. Two direct hits = 116.8 >= 100, so it is a clean 2-shot: 0.800s of trigger time plus flight (22 m/s = 0.68s at 15m), for ~1.48s effective at 15m and ~1.0s at 5m. It can NEVER one-shot (max 70 < 100), which is what keeps it a setup weapon rather than a second rocket. SPLASH: 1.5m off the body column = 70 x (1 - 1.5/3.5) = 40.0, so 3 shots = 1.600s. At 2.5m = 20.0. At 3.5m = 0. THE REAL PAYLOAD: any chip damage sets target.lastDamageAt, which restarts the full SHIELD_RECHARGE_DELAY of 4s. At one shot every 0.8s a single player can suppress an entire flag room's regeneration for five shots straight, and a defense that never regenerates is a defense the 1.0-1.3s utility tier can actually finish. SELF-DAMAGE: explode() has no owner or team exemption, so lobbing into a room you are standing in costs you 40 at 1.5m. That is the counterplay and it stays. Head is not applicable — flat splash, headshotMult 1. NOTE: ctf_pacing quoted 52.0 for a direct hit by citing only d = 0.9; the true worst case under the old feet-measured falloff was 40.4 and its advertised 2-shot was really a 3-shot. E3 makes the 2-shot real.
- **Bots:** The weakest bot fit on any pad, knowingly. Projectiles fly flat here (no gravity on the boomtube/ion_charge branch), so there is no arc to misjudge, but bots still have no velocity lead and 22 m/s is slow enough that a strafing target at 15m is missed by several metres. Two things rescue it: homing:true gives a spawn-time lock inside the 30-degree cone, and 3.5m of splash turns a near-miss into real chip damage plus a regen reset. Score it 2 in the mid bracket and 1 elsewhere so bots do not preferentially hold it, and keep it OFF any pad a bot patrol route crosses once E12 lands.
- **Viewmodel:** launcherParts() — it is kind 'projectile', same family as boomtube and swarm_pod, which is correct for a Cindershot silhouette. IMPORTANT: leave the id in ion_charger's existing WEAPON_ORDER slot (index 7) rather than removing and appending, so its accent hue and pad hologram color stay put.
- **Engine deltas:** Cheapest possible path — a rename with ONE behavioral line, not a new projectile kind. (a) Keep the Projectile kind literal 'ion_charge' untouched so combat.ts's contact branch, projectileKindForWeapon, weaponIdForProjectileKind and the PROJECTILE_LIFETIME detonate-in-place branch all keep working. (b) In explodeProjectile's 'ion_charge' case, replace `applyDamage(target, WEAPONS.ion_charger.damage, now)` with `explode(pr.pos, WEAPONS.cinderlob.damage, WEAPONS.cinderlob.splashRadius ?? 0, playersArr, now)` — verified: the aliveBefore/killPlayer loop after the switch already handles multi-victim kills exactly as it does for boomtube, so kill attribution, killfeed and respawn scheduling all come for free. (c) Rename the id ion_charger -> cinderlob in types.ts, WEAPONS, WEAPON_POOL, WEAPON_ORDER, weaponIdForProjectileKind's return string, projectileKindForWeapon's case, and the tests. Net ~2 changed lines of behavior plus mechanical renames. Drop `kind: 'charge'` and use 'projectile' — 'charge' is a label the engine does not honour and keeping it invites a UI promise of an overcharge that does not exist.

### railspike — S7 Sniper Rifle (S7 Sniper Rifle)

- **Role:** Power pad, 120s, neutral, on the longest sightline (Bastion centre platform). One-shot headshot at any range. Defines every long lane on the map and is the roster's one deliberately human-favoured weapon.
- **Kind:** hitscan
- **Stats:** `{"damage":55,"headshotMult":2,"rof":1,"magSize":4,"pellets":1,"spread":0.002}`
- **TTK math:** rof 1 = 30 ticks = 1.00000s exact. HEAD with headshotIgnoresShield (delta E2): 55 x 2 = 110 >= 100 -> ONE-SHOT KILL from a full shield at any range, 0.000s. Without the flag the gate reads target.shield > 0 and pays mult 1, so a headshot into a full shield does 55 and the Halo sniper simply does not exist — the current weapons.ts comment on railspike documents this as a known regression, so the flag is fixing a bug the repo already logged, not adding a feature. BODY: shot 1 deals 55, shield 70 -> 15, health untouched at 30. Shot 2 deals 55: 15 absorbed, 40 spills into 30 health -> dead. 2 shots, 1 x 1.0 = 1.000s. MIXED (body then head): shot 2 at the now-open gate pays 110, same 1.000s but with enough overkill margin to punch through shield the target regenerated behind cover — a body follow-up only kills while the regained shield is <= 40, a head follow-up kills through a fully restored 70 + 30. Mag 4 = 2 body kills or 4 head kills. NOTE: bot_first's no-delta walk claimed shot 1 leaves 'shield 0 / health 15'; 55 into a 70 shield leaves shield 15 and health 30. Its 2-shot conclusion was right off wrong arithmetic, in the one paragraph justifying its only engine delta.
- **Bots:** Bots hold it competently and can never claim its payoff, and that asymmetry is deliberate — PUT IT IN THE COMMIT MESSAGE so nobody 'fixes' it later. computeAim targets chestPos (PLAYER_BODY_CENTER_Y) and there is no head-aim path anywhere in brain.ts, so a bot with the sniper is a 1.000s two-tap threat, not an instant-death machine. It is also self-limiting for them: 4 degrees of error at 40m is ~2.8m of lateral offset against a 0.58m body radius, and rof 1 makes every miss cost a full second. weaponRangeScore already returns 3 at >25m and 1 at mid, which stays correct. HUMAN-FAVOURED PLACEMENT: put it on the pad that takes the most map knowledge to rotate to, so humans win it on route rather than bots winning it by proximity.
- **Viewmodel:** rifleParts(false), unchanged. Recommend one client addition when E2 lands: a distinct headshot-kill marker and sound. killPlayer already receives `!!hit.head` as its seventh argument, so the data is on the wire — the client just is not spending it, and an unfeedbacked one-shot headshot reads as a bug.
- **Engine deltas:** REQUIRED and the only schema addition in the roster: add `headshotIgnoresShield?: boolean` to WeaponDef, then change one line in stepFire from `const mult = hit.head && target.shield <= 0 ? weapon.headshotMult : 1` to `const mult = hit.head && (target.shield <= 0 || weapon.headshotIgnoresShield) ? weapon.headshotMult : 1`. Two lines. Set it true on railspike ONLY, so the shield-gate invariant stays intact for the entire utility tier. Stat fields: damage 70->55, rof 0.75->1, magSize 5->4, spread 0.004->0.002.

### boomtube — M41 SPNKR (M41 SPNKR Rocket Launcher)

- **Role:** Power pad, 150s, neutral, on the mid route between bases. Multi-kill area denial and the set-defense breaker — the weapon that decides a flag push.
- **Kind:** projectile
- **Stats:** `{"damage":150,"headshotMult":1,"rof":0.6,"magSize":2,"pellets":1,"spread":0,"projectileSpeed":25,"splashRadius":4.5}`
- **TTK math:** rof 0.6 = 50 ticks = 1.66667s exact. All numbers assume delta E3. THE LIVE BUG: stepProjectile detonates within 0.58 of bodyCenter (feet + 0.9) while explode() measures falloff from p.pos, the FEET — so today's 120/3.0 rocket resolves a clean direct hit at 120 x (1 - 1.07/3) = 77, worst case 120 x (1 - 1.48/3) = 60.8. The current SPNKR cannot kill a full-shield defender on a direct hit, which no Halo player would accept. AFTER E3 (falloff measured to the nearest point of the target's body column, so a chest detonation reads d = horizontal offset only): DIRECT HIT worst case d = 0.58 -> 150 x (1 - 0.58/4.5) = 130.7 >= 100, GUARANTEED one-shot with 30 points of margin. SPLASH: guaranteed kill (>=100) within 4.5 x (1 - 100/150) = 1.5m of the body column; full shield strip (>=70) within 2.4m; 4.5m is zero. TWO-ROCKET TTK if both are grazes: 1.667s. Mag 2 then RELOAD_TIME 2s, which is the SPNKR's two-then-reload rhythm. SELF-DAMAGE: no owner exemption in explode(), so a rocket into a wall 2m away costs the shooter 66.7 and rocket-jump risk stays. Head is meaningless (flat splash, headshotMult 1). THIS RESOLVES THE BRACKET'S ONLY REAL DISAGREEMENT: fidelity said 190/4.5 (halo_feel: over-serves, 2.13m no-LOS kill sphere), the other two said 150/4.5 (ctf_math: 100.7 worst case is a coin flip). Both were compensating for the geometry instead of fixing it. 150 + E3 gives 130.7.
- **Bots:** Bots fire it on the generic canFire path with no special-casing, but computeAim targets the current chestPos with no velocity lead, so at 25 m/s a target sprinting laterally at 9.1 m/s is ~5.5m from the aim point after a 0.6s flight at 15m — past the kill radius. The 4.5m splash is the ONLY thing that rescues bot rockets; do not lower it below ~4 or bots become harmless with the best weapon on the map. Score it 2 mid, 1 long. Second-most human-favoured weapon after the sniper: place it where geometry is tight enough that splash forgives — near the mid route, never on an open sightline. KNOWN ACCEPTED GAP: explode() does no LOS test, so 4.5m of splash leaks through thin geometry. Halo blocks splash on walls; fixing it costs one raycast per victim in explode(). Logged, not recommended for v1.
- **Viewmodel:** launcherParts(), unchanged.
- **Engine deltas:** None of its own — 'boomtube' already exists as a Projectile kind with cases in projectileKindForWeapon, weaponIdForProjectileKind, explodeProjectile and the PROJECTILE_LIFETIME detonate-in-place branch, and WEAPONS.boomtube.damage/.splashRadius are already what explodeProjectile passes to explode(). Damage 120->150 and splashRadius 3->4.5 are plain fields. Depends on E3. Hold projectileSpeed at 25 (1.0m of travel per tick against a 1.16m sphere) unless E5 lands.

### arc_blade — Energy Sword (Energy Sword)

- **Role:** Power pad, 120s, in an enclosed side hall on the route between bases — never on an open platform and never inside a flag room. Lunge one-hit kill; owns every corridor it can reach.
- **Kind:** power_melee
- **Stats:** `{"damage":101,"headshotMult":1,"rof":1,"magSize":1,"pellets":1,"spread":0,"lungeRange":5}`
- **TTK math:** rof 1 = 30 ticks = 1.00000s exact. damage is ONE_HIT_KILL_DAMAGE = MAX_SHIELD + MAX_HEALTH + 1 = 101, so any connect inside lungeRange 5 and the 60-degree MELEE_VIEW_CONE kills through a full shield regardless of the shield/health split (applyDamage spills overflow). TTK 0.000s on contact, with no shield-gate interaction at all — power_melee returns before stepFire ever reaches the raycast branch, so body and head are the same swing and headshotMult stays 1 by interface convention. Whiff-to-whiff cadence is 1.000s, which is the counterplay: a missed swing is a full second of free defense. doMeleeAttack builds the cone from viewDir(yaw, 0) — pitch is ignored, so verticality neither helps nor hurts the lunge. BACKSMACK is redundant here (already lethal) but still reports as its own kill type from the target's rear 100-degree cone.
- **Bots:** The single most lethal bot configuration in the game, and it needs no special-casing. Three reasons: target selection is a 60-degree cone pick rather than a raycast, so 4 degrees of aim error is irrelevant; doMeleeAttack lunges at MELEE_LUNGE_SPEED 8 toward the target, closing the gap their pathing leaves; and weaponRangeScore already returns 3 for power_melee under CLOSE_RANGE 4 and 0 above it, so they hard-swap to it exactly where it wins and never try to snipe with it. This is why its pad placement matters more than any other weapon's once E12 lands.
- **Viewmodel:** bladeParts(), unchanged.
- **Engine deltas:** None. rof 1.0 unchanged, lungeRange 5 unchanged, damage stays ONE_HIT_KILL_DAMAGE. Only the WeaponDef.name string changes.

### grav_maul — Gravity Hammer (Gravity Hammer)

- **Role:** Power pad, 150s. NOT on the same map as the Energy Sword — one power melee per map (Bastion takes the Sword, Hairpin and Gutter take the Hammer). Slower, shorter-reach, AREA one-hit kill. The zone tool, not the duelist.
- **Kind:** power_melee
- **Stats:** `{"damage":101,"headshotMult":1,"rof":0.75,"magSize":1,"pellets":1,"spread":0,"lungeRange":3}`
- **TTK math:** rof 0.75 = 40 ticks = 1.33333s exact. ONE_HIT_KILL_DAMAGE 101 -> 0.000s on any connect inside lungeRange 3 and the 60-degree cone, through a full shield. With delta E4 (aoeMelee) the swing kills EVERY live enemy in that cone, not just the nearest — so a two-man flag hold clumped inside 3m dies to one swing. Cadence 1.333s is the longest whiff-punish window in the roster: the Hammer's entire balance is 'if you miss, you die'. Explicitly 2m shorter reach and 0.333s slower than the Sword, so the two are never interchangeable. No headshot or shield-gate math applies (power_melee bypasses the raycast branch entirely).
- **Bots:** Same cone-pick path as the Sword, so bots use it with zero changes and score it 3 under CLOSE_RANGE / 0 above. Slightly weaker for them than the Sword: 3m of reach demands their pathing get closer before the swing lands, and the 1.333s cadence punishes their whiffs harder. The AOE cuts both ways honestly — bot defenses clump around a flag stand and eat it, and bot attackers clump on a push and eat it.
- **Viewmodel:** bladeParts(), unchanged. Worth one client addition with E4: the explosion/impact event on an AOE swing should read wider than a Sword hit, or the multi-kill will look like a desync. Cheapest version is reusing the existing 'explosion' SimEvent at the swing point.
- **Engine deltas:** REQUIRED, ~5 lines, and it is the cheapest remaining fidelity win in the whole tournament: add `aoeMelee?: boolean` to WeaponDef and turn doMeleeAttack's single best-target selection into a loop over every in-cone target when the flag is set (apply damage and the killPlayer call per victim; keep the lunge aimed at the NEAREST target so the movement feel does not change). All three tournament rosters either cut this weapon or shipped it as a shorter, slower Sword, and fidelity priced the fix at 5 lines and then declined it. Infinite's Hammer IS the AOE. Also set lungeRange 3 (it currently leaves the field unset and falls back to MELEE_RANGE 2) and rof 0.7->0.75.

## Engine deltas (E1-E12)

Twelve deltas, smallest first. E1-E4 are required for the roster to behave as specced. E5 is required if any projectileSpeed goes above 20. E6-E11 are registration and data. E12 is the blocker that decides whether this is an 11-weapon sandbox or a 2-weapon one.

E1 — COOLDOWN EPSILON (1 line, shared/src/sim.ts stepFire). Change `if (now < p.cooldownUntil) return` to `if (now < p.cooldownUntil - 1e-6) return`. WHY: server/src/match.ts does `this.simNow += TICK_DT` with TICK_DT = 1/30, so simNow accumulates float error. Every rof in this roster is chosen as 30/n so the shot interval is a whole number of ticks; without the epsilon a cooldown that lands exactly on a tick boundary is a coin flip that silently adds a tick and turns a 1.200s TTK into 1.300s. This delta is what makes the whole table honest. No roster in the bracket caught the quantization at all.

E2 — SNIPER SHIELD-GATE EXEMPTION (2 lines, shared/src/weapons.ts + sim.ts). Add `headshotIgnoresShield?: boolean` to WeaponDef. Change `const mult = hit.head && target.shield <= 0 ? weapon.headshotMult : 1` to `const mult = hit.head && (target.shield <= 0 || weapon.headshotIgnoresShield) ? weapon.headshotMult : 1`. Set true on railspike ONLY. The existing comment block on the shield gate stays true for the entire utility tier. Both losing rosters derived this independently, which corroborates it, and the current weapons.ts comment on railspike already documents the regression it fixes.

E3 — EXPLODE FALLOFF MEASURES TO THE BODY COLUMN (3 lines, shared/src/combat.ts explode()). Today: `const d = length(sub(p.pos, center))` — distance to the target's FEET, while contact detonation happens at bodyCenter (feet + 0.9) up to PLAYER_BODY_RADIUS 0.58 away. Replace with a vertical clamp of the blast point into the target's own body column before measuring: clamp `center.y` to `[p.pos.y, p.pos.y + PLAYER_HEIGHT]`, then measure from `{ x: p.pos.x, y: clampedY, z: p.pos.z }` to `center`. RESULT: a direct chest hit reads d = horizontal offset only (<= 0.58), a grenade at the feet still reads d ~ 0, and a splash 3m away is unchanged. This is why boomtube ships at 150/4.5 instead of the bracket's 190 (over-serves) or bare 150 (100.7 worst case, a coin flip) — and why FRAG_DAMAGE 90 at the feet stays exactly 90, which a naive 'measure to chest' fix would have quietly cut to 69.75. Affects boomtube, cinderlob, frag, mag. Update shared/test/combat.test.ts splash expectations.

E4 — AOE POWER MELEE (~5 lines, shared/src/sim.ts doMeleeAttack). Add `aoeMelee?: boolean` to WeaponDef. When set, replace the single best-target selection with a loop over every in-cone, in-range live target (applyDamage + the killPlayer call per victim); keep the lunge aimed at the nearest target so movement feel is untouched. Set true on grav_maul only. Backsmack logic stays per-victim and is a no-op for a weapon already dealing ONE_HIT_KILL_DAMAGE.

E5 — SWEPT PROJECTILE COLLISION (~6 lines, shared/src/combat.ts stepProjectile). Today the boomtube/ion_charge, swarm_dart and mag contact branches all point-sample `distSq(pr.pos, bodyCenter(p.pos)) <= PLAYER_BODY_RADIUS^2` AFTER advancing pos by vel*dt. At TICK_DT 1/30 a 24 m/s needle moves 0.8m and a 25 m/s rocket moves 1.0m per tick against a 1.16m-diameter sphere, so a glancing trajectory passes clean through and registers nothing. `prevPos` is already captured on the line above, so this is a segment-vs-sphere sweep between prevPos and pr.pos, not new state. REQUIRED if swarm_pod goes to 24 (six discrete sticks are needed for the supercombine, so dropped sticks are not cosmetic). If E5 is deferred, hold every projectileSpeed at 20 and re-derive the needler flight times. No roster caught this.

E6 — CINDERSHOT (2 changed lines + renames, shared/src/sim.ts explodeProjectile). In the 'ion_charge' case, replace `applyDamage(target, WEAPONS.ion_charger.damage, now)` with `explode(pr.pos, WEAPONS.cinderlob.damage, WEAPONS.cinderlob.splashRadius ?? 0, playersArr, now)`. Verified: the aliveBefore/killPlayer loop after the switch already handles multi-victim kills exactly as it does for boomtube, so killfeed, attribution and respawn scheduling come free. KEEP the Projectile kind literal 'ion_charge' so combat.ts's contact branch, projectileKindForWeapon, weaponIdForProjectileKind and the PROJECTILE_LIFETIME detonate-in-place branch all keep compiling. Rename the WeaponId ion_charger -> cinderlob in types.ts, WEAPONS, WEAPON_POOL, WEAPON_ORDER (in place, same index), the two switch cases and the tests, and change kind 'charge' -> 'projectile'. WARNING from the bracket: bot_first proposed DELETING ion_charger outright and called it a registration edit — it is a compile break across four sites, and this rename-in-place is why the Cindershot is cheaper than the Plasma Pistol it replaces.

E7 — FIXED SPAWN LOADOUT (~15 lines net deleted, shared/src/weapons.ts). rollLoadout keeps its signature and return shape; see loadoutRules. Delete RANGED_POOL and GRENADE_SPLITS.

E8 — NEW WEAPON ID 'commando' (registration only). WeaponId union in types.ts, WEAPONS entry, WEAPON_POOL, WEAPON_ORDER append in viewmodel.ts (AFTER E10), weaponRangeScore branch in brain.ts, map pad tables. Hitscan, so zero projectile plumbing.

E9 — BOT WEAPON SCORING (4 lines, server/src/bots/brain.ts weaponRangeScore). All four are bug fixes, not tunes: (a) `if (id === 'scattergun') return 0` in the >LONG_RANGE branch — today it falls through to the generic 1, so a bot holding a 15m-capped shotgun at 30m keeps it out and fires into empty air; (b) add `sidearm` to the LONG_RANGE score-3 branch — today the 45m-capped AR and the Sidekick both score 1, desiredWeaponSlot ties, and the bot strands itself with the AR; (c) add `commando` to the same branch; (d) `if (id === 'cinderlob') return 1` in the LONG branch and 2 at mid, so bots do not preferentially hold the roster's worst bot weapon.

E10 — ACCENT HUE STABILITY (1 line, client/src/render/viewmodel.ts). `accentColor` is `setHSL(idx / WEAPON_ORDER.length, ...)`, so appending 'commando' takes the divisor from 10 to 11 and re-hues EVERY existing weapon's viewmodel accent and its pickup-pad glow/hologram. Switch the divisor to a fixed constant (12, leaving headroom) before appending. bot_first was the only roster to spot the index dependency, and it then wrongly claimed appending at the end was safe — growing the array re-hues everything either way.

E11 — MAP PAD TABLES (data only, shared/src/maps/{bastion,hairpin,gutter}.ts). Verified: `pickupAvailableAt[i] ?? 0` is a sparse lookup, so growing powerPickups needs no other code.

E12 — BOT PAD-SEEKING (the blocker, server/src/bots/brain.ts + nav.ts). stepPickups says it outright: "Bots ignore pads for now -- they never path to one, they can only collect one they happen to walk across." I grepped all of server/src/bots and confirmed zero pickup logic. WITHOUT THIS, every seeded bot match is fought with AR + Sidekick and all 9 pad weapons are a human-only layer. Cheapest honest version: in the brain's goal selection, when the bot has no combat target and is not carrying or chasing a flag, score the nearest pad whose `pickupAvailableAt[i] <= now` within a detour budget (~15m off the current goal path) and push its pos to the Navigator; drop the goal the moment a target appears. Gate power pads behind the role check so runners do not abandon a flag push for a sniper. IF THIS IS DEFERRED, say so in the roster doc and in the commit: the niche and power tiers are human-only for v1. Do not ship the per-weapon 'bots: yes' notes above without that line.

## HUD notes

WHAT IS FREE. weaponDisplayName reads WEAPONS[id].name directly, so every rename in this roster — Pulse SMG to MA40 Assault Rifle, Railspike to S7 Sniper Rifle, and the rest — costs zero HUD code. The ammo row already renders "N/magSize" or "RELOADING" from live state, and power_melee never reaches the reload path in stepFire, so the Sword and Hammer correctly never show RELOADING despite magSize 1. The reload dip animation is kind-agnostic and needs no per-weapon work.

WHAT BREAKS IF YOU ARE NOT CAREFUL. The single id rename ion_charger -> cinderlob is the only HUD-visible identity change; grep the killfeed and any weapon-name map for the literal string before assuming WEAPONS[id].name covers it. accentColor() drives BOTH the viewmodel accent and the pickup pad glow/hologram, so delta E10 (fixed divisor) must land before 'commando' is appended or every pad on every map changes color in the same commit that adds one gun — a cosmetic churn that will read as a rendering bug in review.

WHAT THE ROSTER ADDS AND THE CLIENT MUST SPEND.
1. HEADSHOT KILL FEEDBACK (required with E2). killPlayer already receives `!!hit.head` as its seventh argument — the data is on the wire and the client is not spending it. A sniper that one-shots a full-shield player through the head with the same marker and sound as a body shot reads as a bug, not a skill payoff. Give the headshot kill its own marker and audio cue. This is the cheapest legibility win in the change.
2. AOE HAMMER FEEDBACK (required with E4). One swing killing two players will look like a desync unless the impact reads wider than a Sword hit. Cheapest version: reuse the existing 'explosion' SimEvent at the swing point.
3. PAD STATE IS THE HUMAN SURFACE OF THIS ENTIRE DESIGN. Nine of eleven weapons live behind timed pads, and a pad system a player cannot read is a pad system that does not exist — the whole rotation game is invisible. Minimum viable: the pad hologram already exists, so make an unavailable pad visibly dead and show its remaining respawnSec on approach. sim.pickupAvailableAt is already server state; it needs to reach the snapshot. Without this, the power tier is a thing that happens to players rather than a thing they contest, and the strategic thesis of the roster never lands for a human at all.
4. The 'pickup' SimEvent already fires on collection; surface it as a brief on-screen weapon-name callout so a player knows what they just walked over — stepPickups replaces the ACTIVE slot, which is a surprising swap if it happens silently mid-fight.

WHAT NOT TO BUILD. Do not promise an overcharge in any UI text or tooltip. There is no hold-to-charge mechanic anywhere in the engine; kind 'charge' is executed by byte-identical code to 'projectile'. That is precisely why the Plasma Pistol was cut and the Cindershot took its slot.

MATH THE JUDGES FLAGGED, AND WHAT WAS DONE ABOUT IT:
- TICK QUANTIZATION (engine_fit, and it invalidated numbers in all three rosters). cooldownUntil = now + 1/rof resolved on a 30Hz grid means the real interval is ceil(30/rof) ticks. fidelity's rof 9 AR was really 1.600s, not 1.333s; ctf_pacing's rof 12 SMG was really 10/s. EVERY rof in the final table is now 30/n for an integer n, plus a one-line epsilon on the cooldown gate so float drift in simNow cannot silently add a tick.
- THE ROCKET (halo_feel said fidelity's 190 over-serves; ctf_math said 150 is a coin flip at 100.7). Both were compensating for geometry with a damage number. Fixed at the source instead: explode() now measures to the nearest point of the target's body column rather than their feet, which makes 150/4.5 a guaranteed 130.7 direct hit with a clean 1.5m lethal bubble AND leaves frag/mag grenade tuning untouched. fidelity's own "64 worst case" for the current build was also wrong — it is 60.8.
